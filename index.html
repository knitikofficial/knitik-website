Introduction

KNITIK ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Information we collect

  • Details you share with us: name, phone number, email, business name and business details, usually via WhatsApp, phone or forms.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide our services.
  • To send proposals, reports and service updates.
  • To improve our website and measure our own marketing.

Cookies and tracking

We may use tools such as Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand website traffic and the performance of our ads. These load only after you accept cookies, and you can decline at any time.

Sharing with third parties

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers we need to run our business, such as advertising platforms, hosting, analytics and communication tools, and only to the extent required.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, by contacting us using the details below.

Children's privacy

Our services are meant for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
