1. Introduction

Welcome to KNITIK ("we," "our," or "us"). KNITIK is a specialized performance marketing and growth agency operating from Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India. We are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of your personal data in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act), and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website (knitik.com), interact with our marketing advertisements, submit consultation inquiries, or engage with our services via WhatsApp and phone.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us as well as data gathered automatically when navigating our site:

  • Direct Inquiries & Form Submissions: Full name, telephone/WhatsApp number, corporate email address, business name, industry sector, current monthly ad spend, and campaign requirements submitted via forms or direct messages.
  • Communication Data: Inquiries, call notes, and correspondence exchanged through WhatsApp Business, email, Google Meet, or telephonic discussions.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, operating system versions, referral URLs, time zone settings, and interaction logs collected through cookies and analytical scripts.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data strictly for lawful, defined commercial purposes, including:

  • Evaluating your business requirements to prepare tailored growth audits and marketing strategy proposals.
  • Initiating requested contact via WhatsApp, phone call, or email to schedule strategy consultations.
  • Executing agreed digital marketing, Meta ad campaigns, web development, and automation workflows.
  • Optimizing website performance, conversion tracking, user experience, and testing creative performance.
  • Complying with statutory accounting, taxation, and legal requirements under Indian law.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We employ standard cookies, web beacons, and tracking pixels (such as the Meta Pixel, Meta Conversions API, and Google Analytics 4) to evaluate traffic attribution and measure ad performance.

In accordance with our cookie consent mechanism, analytical and marketing tracking pixels are loaded strictly after you provide your consent or continue browsing under our policy terms. You may disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; however, some site functionalities may become restricted.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data. We may share data strictly with trusted service providers under non-disclosure obligations, solely to facilitate business operations:

  • Advertising & Attribution Platforms: Meta Platforms Inc. (Facebook, Instagram), Google LLC for conversion tracking and ad campaign management.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Communication: Secure hosting servers, CRM software (Make.com, Calendly), and WhatsApp Business API providers.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: Where mandated by law, court summons, or governmental directives under Indian jurisdiction.

6. Data Security

We employ industry-standard technical, administrative, and physical security safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of personal data. This includes SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit, multi-factor authentication on internal dashboards, and restricted team access protocols.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes outlined in this policy, or to satisfy legal, tax, or regulatory dispute-resolution mandates under the laws of India. When data is no longer needed, it is permanently deleted or anonymized.

8. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you hold the following rights as a Data Principal:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of your personal data processed by us.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You can request that inaccurate data be updated or that your personal data be erased from our systems.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may revoke consent for ongoing marketing communications at any point.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You may lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer regarding any data concern.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are targeted exclusively at adult business owners, entrepreneurs, and corporate representatives aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit data from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological advances or changes in statutory law. The revised version will always indicate the updated date at the top of the page.
