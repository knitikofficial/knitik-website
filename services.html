SERVICE QUESTIONS

Frequently asked service questions

Can I hire KNITIK for just one specific service? +

Yes. While our clients experience the highest compounding ROI when combining Meta Ads with our conversion web development and WhatsApp automations, you are welcome to hire us for individual specialized services such as Meta Ads management or custom landing page funnels.

Who owns the ad account and creative assets? +

You do. 100% of the ad accounts, custom audiences, pixel data, website code, and creative assets belong exclusively to you. We simply connect as an agency partner with manager-level access.

How does billing and ad spend work? +

KNITIK charges a flat monthly management fee or performance milestone fee billed in advance. Your ad spend is paid directly to Meta/Facebook via your own credit card or corporate payment method on file.

What contract length is required? +

We work on 90-day initial agreements because performance optimization requires sufficient testing and scaling iterations. Following the initial period, agreements roll over month-to-month with complete peace of mind.
