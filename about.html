OUR STORY

The KNITIK Philosophy

KNITIK was founded with a singular conviction: Indian businesses deserve digital marketing that directly translates to cash collected, not inflated vanity metrics.

Too many founders were getting burned by agencies charging heavy monthly retainers while reporting meaningless impressions, clicks, and likes. We set out to build an agency that acts as an invested co-pilot — architecting full customer acquisition engines where ad spend, creative copywriting, conversion-focused websites, and automated lead nurturing work as one seamless machine.

Our Mission To help Indian businesses grow with honest, data-driven marketing.
Our Standard Track every rupee. Optimize for pipeline, not impressions.
